How to get a user token
  1. Open discord.com in your browser and sign in
  2. Press F12 → open the Console tab
  3. Run: localStorage.getItem("token").replaceAll('"','')
  4. Copy the result and paste it here

⚠️ The token grants full access to your account. Never share it. Third-party clients violate Discord's Terms of Service.
